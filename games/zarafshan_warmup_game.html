Water flows from left to right; the arrowheads show the direction. A dashed arrow that ends in the open is water that leaves to the air: evaporation, and ET (evapotranspiration), the water the crops use. Numbers on the lines are Mm³ (million m³) that flowed in the year or month shown; the numbers at the reservoir and at the two Drainage blocks are Mm³ stored (in the year view: on 1 October and on 30 September). The line width shows the flow in m³/s. Dashed lines lead to and from the reservoir. A thin dotted line carries no water. The column in the Reservoir box shows its storage. Each Drainage block is the ground under the fields above it: the water that drains from them returns to the river months later. Each user's box says what it needs. At a canal, “a of b” means: the canal took a of the b it needs, including what is lost and what drains away. The river line gets wider where the power plant and the Drainage block give water back. The numbers under Inflow and River end are what enters and what leaves the model. ✓ supplied, ✕ not fully supplied. Click a block to see what kind of TaqSim block it is and what it does.

Levers

Each lever changes one number in a block's rule. The full game has levers with these names; there the release share serves the Kattakurgan command only.

Your tasks

After this game you can:

  • name TaqSim's building blocks and say which one is a river, a canal, a reservoir, a headworks;
  • say why, in a dry year, the crops, the river downstream and next year's storage cannot all get what they need;
  • read a plot of plans that no other plan beats.

    The ticks only help you find your way. This is not a test. Next: the full Zarafshan game.
